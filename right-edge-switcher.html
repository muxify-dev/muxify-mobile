Muxify Mobile · Debrief · Task “Switch destinations with a right-edge hold” · second edition

The Grip and the Edge Drawer are built, then reshaped on the iPhone and the iPad. They are now a glass line and a drag-only view of the place on screen.

Question: What does the build do now, after the six decisions and the feedback on the devices, and what differs from what was asked?

Answer: A 400 ms hold on a small Liquid Glass line at the right edge opens a floating glass panel. It lists only the Topic or Session on screen, from the bottom up. You slide onto a row and lift to go there; every lift closes the panel. Three decisions changed by Gleb's feedback: D2 (now drag only), D3 (no other Topics or Sessions) and D6 (the title tap stays). Not done: a page scroll and a tap through the band, Panes of other Windows, the iPad pointer, and the device suite.

State of the work: not committed, on master in /Users/glebglazov/private/Dev/work/muxify-mobile. 10 new files and 10 changed files. Sources: the decided planning page, the saved Task, and Gleb's messages in the session. The latest Release build is on the iPhone. Section 3 · How a touch on the Grip goes

One finger decides: a drag before 400 ms is a Pane Swipe; after the hold, a lift on a row picks it and every other lift picks nothing

Example: Gleb is on Window 0 of his iPhone. He puts his thumb on the line in the middle of the right edge. The line fills with colour. At 400 ms the phone ticks, the line glows, and the panel slides in with his Session's card at the bottom. He slides left onto “1 logs”, which lights, and lifts. The panel closes at once, and the Attach goes to Window 1. Had he lifted where he started, in the Lift Lane, the panel would close and nothing would be picked.

The rules are pure functions in src/navigation/grip.ts: moveGrip (line 79), liftGrip (line 115) and autoScrollSpeed (line 147). The view EdgeGrip runs the timer and keeps the finger once the panel opens, so a scroll under it cannot take the touch (src/components/edge-grip.tsx:132). VoiceOver has no held finger: its activate on the Grip opens a panel that stays open (:129).

What makes it iOS-like

A rounded Liquid Glass panel that floats 8 pt in from the edges. The title is the Topic, or the Session when the Attach holds none, with a small TOPIC or SESSION above it. Each Session has a grey header and a rounded card. Rows have SF Symbols, inset separators, and a checkmark on the current Window and Pane. A lift on a Session's header goes to that Session.

Why the order is bottom up

The thumb opens the panel at the right edge, low on the screen. drawerLayout puts the other Sessions first and the attached one last (src/components/edge-drawer.tsx:333). Inside a card the order is the Title Sheet's. The list keeps its distance from the end as it lays out, so it opens at its end, and Tabs that arrive later do not move the rows under the finger (:77).

Section 5 · What differs, and why

Four differences have four causes: the native Surface, React Native's touches, the Topology, and drag only

P1, P2 · No page scroll and no tap through the band

The Surface's gestures are native, in the local patch to expo-libghostty. A recognizer of the app's own over the Surface loses the race to them, as the MouseLayer comment in terminal.tsx records. So the band is a view that takes every touch that lands in it.

P4 · No pointer hover or click

React Native gives the band a touch for a trackpad click, with no sign that it is a pointer. A click must also be held 400 ms.

A3, P7 · Panes of the Window on screen only

The Topology holds the Panes of the active Window only. A Pane in another Window is two picks.

F10 · The drawer closes at the pick, and a failed move says so only in the log

Before, a Window or Session pick kept the drawer open until the move ended, to show “Going…” or an error. The open panel covered the Grip, and on a phone its scrim covered the screen. After a failed move it stayed open with no close button. Now leaveDrawer closes it and keeps the pick's revision, so the move goes on (src/hooks/use-title-sheet.ts:370). A failure goes to the device log (:294). The glass is also not interactive any more, since its own touch handling could take the touch. That these were the only causes is not proven.

Section 7 · What no item asked

Six additions came from the design, not from a request

  • A Tab of another Window goes to its Window first, so the Tab is in the Place of its own Window and the next restore keeps it.
  • The drawer is the Title Sheet's second presentation (via: 'sheet' | 'drawer', src/hooks/use-title-sheet.ts:44), so the picks are the sheet's own.
  • A tick when the panel opens, VoiceOver's activate on the Grip, and the escape gesture to close the panel.
  • The autoscroll waits until the finger leaves the Grip, because the finger opens the panel near the list's bottom edge.
  • A lift on a Session's header goes to that Session. It replaces the Session row of the first build.
  • An e2e suite, an ADR and five glossary terms: e2e/edge-drawer.sh, ADR 0036, CONTEXT.md:723. markSymbol is exported from src/components/topic-marks.tsx:32, and the Key Row's onPanes is optional (src/components/key-row.tsx:55).
